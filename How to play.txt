EMBERVALE — The Fading Flame
============================

HOW TO PLAY
  Double-click "index.html". It opens in your web browser (Chrome, Edge,
  Firefox or Safari). No install and no internet needed.

  New players start with a short tutorial (Chapter 0) that teaches everything.
  Press H in-game for the Wiki, or Esc for the Menu.

CONTROLS
  WASD move  •  Mouse aim  •  Left-click attack  •  Space dodge
  Q E R F skills  •  V Ember Surge  •  1 potion  •  Tab swap weapon
  I inventory  •  K skills  •  M map  •  Esc menu  •  H wiki
  E (in Haven) talk to villagers  •  Click the minimap to zoom
  All combat keys can be changed in Settings -> Controls.

THINGS TO DISCOVER
  Nemesis monsters, real-time villager Expeditions, Weekly Hunts,
  the Collector's Ledger and Renown ranks, weapon Lineage, Challenge
  Totems, pet evolution and kill streaks.
  11 item rarities, from Common up to the near-mythical Eternal
  (Ancient and above carry two legendary powers), item Ascension,
  20 weapon types, each with its own signature Art and Mastery track,
  elemental weapon infusions, weapon Awakening, Light/Medium/Heavy armor,
  armor tempering, item sets, crafting at Brann's workbench, skill runes, regional floor
  hazards, secret rooms behind cracked walls, Ember Braziers, caged
  villagers to rescue, Haven requests, the Guardian Gauntlet and the
  Weekly Invasion. The Wiki (H) explains every one of them.

SAVES
  Progress saves automatically in your browser (3 save slots).
  Use "Save Slots -> Export" on the title screen to back up a character
  or move it to another computer, and "Import save" to load it.
  "Reset all progress" on the title screen wipes everything.
